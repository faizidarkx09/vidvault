Public instances are shared, so heavy traffic can briefly throttle them — wait a minute and retry. Some mobile networks also block certain instances; VidVault auto-tries several, and if none respond you'll get a one-tap button that opens the official cobalt.tools site with your link pre-filled. For uninterrupted use, self-host your own instance with the one-line docker command in Settings.

Downloading content you own, that's Creative Commons, or that you have permission to save is fine. For everything else, respect the creator's rights and the platform's terms — VidVault is a tool; how you use it is your responsibility.

Your link goes straight from your browser to the download engine, which processes it in memory. VidVault itself has no server and no database — your history is kept only in your browser's local storage.
